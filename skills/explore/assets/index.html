Think with your agent.

Open Explore in Codex, Claude Code, or Copilot and ask it to continue this workspace. Keep that agent session active while you explore.

Your agent waits for new work only while its session stays open. A longer wait keeps that session busy and uses more of its context.

The site saves your ideas and queues selected branches. It doesn’t run a model on its own. Queued work waits safely while your agent is away.

Export

Keep a copy to import again, or put the map in documents, notes and slides. Each idea shows whether its findings are reviewed.

Include

Choose the ideas to share; nothing is chosen at first. For each, choose whether its answers and its findings and sources go in. The file opens in any browser, offline, and can be searched. It holds up to 500 ideas and 4 MiB.
